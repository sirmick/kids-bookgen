# Nighttime Books — content plan

Target: Bigme HiBreak, 1072×1448 colour e-ink. Ages 3–6. Twelve pages per book.
Images: full-bleed 3:4, generated with Nano Banana (Gemini flash image) via OpenRouter,
one character-sheet image per book passed as a reference into every page call.
Text is drawn by the app in a solid white panel; default position is the bottom
quarter, `[top]` marks pages where the composition wants it at the top instead.

Page 12 is always the sleeping page. Forward on page 12 returns to the library.

## Shared style sheet (goes into every image prompt)

Children's picture-book illustration for ages 3 to 6. Flat, hand-drawn look with soft,
confident black outlines and gentle rounded shapes. Limited palette of five muted colours
plus cream. Flat fills, no gradients, no shading, no texture, no photorealism. Backgrounds
are pale and mostly empty. Dark colours only in outlines and small accents, never as large
areas. Warm, calm, sleepy mood. No text, letters or numbers anywhere in the image.

A book may override the shared style with its own **Style:** bullet.
Per-page prompts add: the book's palette, the sky for that time of evening, the
character(s) present, and the beat below.

## Notes for the script

- One character sheet per book: the main character from the front, side and curled-up
  asleep, on plain cream, in the shared style. Reference image for every page.
- Secondary animals do not need consistency across pages; each appears once.
- Page text is final copy, not a prompt. The script only generates images.
- Palette words go into the prompt verbatim; the sky arc gives the per-page sky colour.
